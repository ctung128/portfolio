Huiwen Mincho (汇文明朝体), version GBK 1.001

Bundled here as huiwen-mincho-hero.woff2: a subset of the full font holding only the
hero's Chinese name (童雪玲). Rebuild it when that text changes:

  pyftsubset ~/Library/Fonts/汇文明朝体.otf --text="童雪玲" --flavor=woff2 \
    --output-file=src/fonts/huiwen-mincho-hero.woff2
